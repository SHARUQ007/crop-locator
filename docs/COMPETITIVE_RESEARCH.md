# CropLocator: product and interaction research

Research date: 7 November 2026. Research used public primary product documentation, help articles and product screenshots. It did not involve logged-in competitor accounts. The implementation recommendations below are design inferences, distinct from sourced competitor capabilities.

## Product context from the supplied material

The two SBSF leaflets describe species-level screening using crop occurrence and FloraKB requirements, approximately 2,000 SBSF-defined AEZs, 100+ environmental variables, a production 9 × 9 km grid and 1 × 1 km under development. AEZ colours represent environmental classes, not suitability. Local evidence and variety trials refine screening. The persona notes prioritise commodity sourcing, seed businesses, agricultural finance, estates and ingredient buyers, with research as another use case.

The prototype therefore keeps environmental fit, commercial assumptions, source provenance and local evidence separate. It does not reproduce unsupported headline yield-uplift or emissions-reduction claims from reference screenshots.

## Geospatial and technical references

- [FAO GAEZ](https://www.fao.org/gaez/en) and [what is new in GAEZ v5](https://www.fao.org/statistics/highlights-archive/highlights-detail/what-s-new-in-gaez-v5/en): approximately 1 km key suitability/yield outputs, climate baselines, future periods and management conditions support a crop × cell × time/scenario model. FAO values are **not** used in this synthetic dataset.
- [Leaflet reference](https://leafletjs.com/reference): interactive map, raster layers, vector cell overlays, linked markers and attribution.
- [OpenStreetMap tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/): browser-only interactive requests, visible attribution, normal caching and referrer behaviour. No tile scraping, bulk download or offline tile packaging. Production hosting should use a contracted basemap provider with an appropriate capacity agreement.

## Primary journeys

1. Select persona, workspace, first objective, crop and starting region; optionally simulate a project-scoped teammate invitation.
2. Find land for a crop, or rank crops for a sample land location. Keep crop, season, water regime and time context explicit.
3. Inspect a candidate: environmental fit first, then reveal climate, soil, economics or evidence as needed.
4. Add up to three candidates to comparison; change the climate period and compare under shared assumptions.
5. Save crop/location candidates and their context to a project. Save economic assumptions as a scenario.
6. Add evidence via manual entry or validated CSV import. Record the decision rationale and export a printable brief.
7. Manage mock members, role descriptions and project access without implying a connected invitation or authentication service.

## Deliberate prototype boundaries

This is a functional frontend with browser-local persistence, not a live agronomy engine. Supplier verification, live market prices, detailed procurement contracts, loan/insurance decisions, variety-level prediction, farm-system integrations, real shared collaboration, authenticated permissions and model retraining are not represented as connected capabilities. Environmental confidence is a synthetic index. No production claim is made for 1 km SBSF data.

## Global discovery and location defaults

The final implementation supports regional defaults and deliberate worldwide exploration. The location picker searches countries, administrative regions, sample locations and AEZ identifiers. Its default algorithm uses a saved choice first, already-permitted device coordinates second, a supported device-timezone country third, then a manual picker. It does not request GPS permission without a user action, persist precise coordinates, or silently force Worldwide.

Selecting a location compacts the map and scrolls the map/details pair into view. A sticky discovery bar and a strong selected-location action make the full-width Location Intelligence section discoverable. The map remains above it.

Three exploration lenses distinguish suitability, growing origins and AEZ analogues. Suitability is synthetic. A country-level growing reference does not establish cultivation at the selected sample point. Environmental class similarity does not establish transferable variety performance.
